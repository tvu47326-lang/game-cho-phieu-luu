DEVELOPER'S NOTE

A GAME BY ONE DEVELOPER

Chợ Phiêu Lưu

Created by Vu Thanh Tuan · Developed in 2026

Hello everyone! I am Vu Thanh Tuan, the sole developer of Chợ Phiêu Lưu. I built this game from the ground up, including its changing daily economy, customer relationships, cooking system and shop management.

My hope is to give you the feeling of building something from nothing: buying wisely, keeping customers happy, paying your expenses and slowly growing a small riverside shop into a successful business.

Thank you for playing, for being patient with the early bugs, and for giving me the chance to prove myself. Thank you all so, so much!
